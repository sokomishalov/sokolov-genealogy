  Summary charts prepared by Dr. Stumpp showing population totals for the

Regierungsbezirk Westpreussen und Freistaat Danzig mit besonderer

as a discussion of Mennonite churches there.  A short list of gravestone
inscriptions from the Elbing-Ellerwald Mennonite Church cemetery is given.
