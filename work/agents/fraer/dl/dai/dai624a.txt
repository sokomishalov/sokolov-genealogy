NOTES:

The data below is taken from the DAI film T81-624, frames 5420560-0571.

This is a report supplied by Dr. Hermann Roemer, Markgroeningen, concerning
research of families reflected in the State Archives in Ludwigsburg, and those
Schwaben persons and families leaving from the reflected communities.

We need to have some translator volunteer to read the words that go along with
this data so we better understand the full meaning of this data.

This film (624) will be added to the collection at the GRHS film library in
Bismarck ND.  However there is other work being done concerning this film, and
until that work is done, the film will not be found there.

This data is a product of the GRHS Clearing House DAI project.
