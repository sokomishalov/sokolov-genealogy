National Archives Publication T81 Film 610 National Socialist German Labor Party 
(NSDAP) and the Deutsches Ausland-Institut (DAI), Stuttgart

Finding aid No. 21 says the following (in part) about rolls 609 and 610: "DAI 
files containing correspondence and questionnaires, filled out by 
Russlanddeutsche, and Ahnentafel, 1940. Some interesting material is found in 
biographical additions to the Ahnentafel. The files also contain a list of the 
Russlanddeutsche communities in South America and a description of their 
condition, probably from 1936-1937." (suggest you read Russlanddeutsche as 
German Russian)

NOTE:
It is intended that this film be incorporated into the German Russian collection 
of films held at GRHS Headquarters in Bismarck ND.  Copies can be obtained from 
the National Archives.

There exists far too much data on this film to completely Index everything.  
Many of the items that were indexed will only take you to a portion of the film 
- where the researcher will need to ensure they look around for other family 
data as well or more details about the person that brought them to this film.

It should also be noted that persons with birth dates were keyed for extraction 
into this index.  So you will find many entries within this massive data that 
was not indexed because of missing birth date information.
