It is intended that this film be incorporated into the German Russian 
collection of films held at GRHS Headquarters in Bismarck ND.  Copies can be 
obtained from the National Archives.

We found on film 302 of the T81 DAI records that we had a definition of church 
records by notes of Karl Stumpp as they existed in Bessarabia in 1941.

We have tried to index this data to include most all of the information that is 
on the notes.  There is however other data on this film relative to these notes 
and with time we will further clarify more what these notes were all about.

For now, the person who is interested in the Bessarabian church records can use 
this list to understand what apparently existed in 1941 and to also see the 
results of a matching with the LDS church records to see what we already have 
in the LDS inventory and what we don't at the time of this list.

(When performing the "matching" of this listed data with the LDS inventory, 
there may not be a complete overlapping of dates in each instance, check the 
LDS Bessarabian film list found posted here at this site also.)

It should be noted that we do know for a fact that the LDS are processing other 
Bessarabian Church records which will become available very soon in their 
inventory. But until those film numbers are made available to the public along 
with the record description, we cannot reflect them as being in the system yet.  
So stay tuned to news about new Bessarabian church records.

Also in this same data, there are listings of other records other than church 
records.  We have a continuing project at the GRHS Clearing House to further 
process this data and make it available to the Bessarabian researcher.
