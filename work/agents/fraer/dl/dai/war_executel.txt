During the early 1990s, Neues Leben, a German language newspaper in Moscow,
published a series of lists of ethnic Germans executed between August 1937 
and November 1938.  The first list was published in issues No. 38-43 and 
46-49 between 18 September 1991 and 4 December 1991.  This list included 
1208 individuals numbered from 23 through 4374.  Not all numbers are used 
and the significance of the individual number is not known.  This first 
known list was translated from Russian by Harold Ehrman.

A second list was published in Neues Leben issue No. 25 on 17 June 1992.  
This list includes 519 individuals numbered 6769 through 7972.  Again, not 
all numbers were used. This second list was translated by Ray Heinle.  The
two lists are combined and sorted by surname in this document.
