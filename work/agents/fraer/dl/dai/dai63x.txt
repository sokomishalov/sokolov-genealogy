NOTES:

These are WWI Fragebogen dated 1918, rather than the WWII exodus Fragebogen
that most of us are familiar with.

It is intended that these films be incorporated into the German Russian
collection of films held at GRHS Headquarters in Bismarck ND.  Copies can be
obtained from the National Archives.

There exists far too much data on these films to completely index everything.
Many of the items that were indexed will only take you to a frame of the film -
where the researcher should find more details about the person/individual.

Following is a more technical description of what can be found on these films.

National Archives Publication T81 Film 630, 631 and 632.

Provenance: Deutsches Ausland-Institut (DAI), (Deutsches Ausland-Museum).

Finding aid No. 21 applies; Three folders of DAI containing questionnaires
filled out by Russlanddeutsche, 1918, and marked "Deutsches Ausland" Museum and
"Fragebogen fuer Deutsche im neutralen Ausland." The questionnaires give
information pertaining to birthplace, social origin, occupation, religious
confession, number of children, etc., of the persons involved, and also
observations pertinent to the Russian Revolution.  The folders are alphabetized
A-Hi, Ho-O, P-Z, respectively.
