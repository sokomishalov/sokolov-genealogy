        This file contains birth records from the parish of Odessa
        (the city). Various and random years of the St Petersburg
        reports are included in this single file.  This information
        was assembled by several people and coordinated by the GRHS
        Village Research Project Clearing House in 1995/6.  The
        data source is the St. Petersburg Lutheran Evangelical
        archives published by the LDS.  It was determined that
        because this project is progressing so slowly, we will
        periodically add new years data as it becomes available.
        As whole decade's of data become digitized, we will
        then break those decades off from these basic random years.
        Edited by Marty McMahon.

        Anyone desiring to help with this project, can contact the
        GRHS Clearing House (contact the office in Bismarck).
        -dw (5-31-1996)
