           This file contains birth records from the village of Neu
           Glueckstal.  Neu Glueckstal was first a daughter colony
           of the Glueckstal Colonies that was founded just south
           of Hoffnungstal. Later the village was part of the
           Hoffnungstal parish.  All the years in the St Petersburg
           reports are included in this one file.  This information
           was compiled by Dale Wahl in 1995/6 from the St. Petersburg
           Lutheran Evangelical archives published by the LDS. Edited
           by Marty McMahon.  -dw (5-31-1996)
