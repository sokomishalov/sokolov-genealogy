           This file contains marriage records from the parish
           of Odessa (the city), South Russia for the decade 188x.
           This information is being assembled by several people,
           as coordinated by the GRHS Village Research Project
           Clearing House, from the St. Petersburg Lutheran Evangelical
           archives published by the LDS.  It was determined that
           because this project is progressing so slowly, we will
           periodically add new years data as it becomes available.

           Anyone desiring to help with this project, can contact
           the GRHS Clearing House (contact the office in Bismarck).
           Edited by Marty McMahon.
