        This file contains marriage records from the Parishes
           of Volhynia, South Russia for the decade 183x.
           This information was compiled by Marcia Momose and 
           coworkers January, 1998 from the St. Petersburg
           Lutheran Evangelical archives published by the LDS
           and edited by Marty McMahon.

           Please take some time and review the history file
           in this Volhynia - St. Petersburg directory to gain
           insight into the history and a list of the team 
           working on this project.
