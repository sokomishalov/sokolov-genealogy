This file contains marriage records from the Parishes of Volhynia, South Russia 
for the years 1876-1879. This information was compiled by Marcia Momose and 
coworkers December 2000, from the St. Petersburg Lutheran Evangelical archives 
published by the LDS.

Please take some time and review the General file in this Volhynia - St. 
Petersburg directory to gain insight into the history and a list of the team 
working on this project.
