           This file contains birth records from the village of Neu
           Beresina.  Neu Beresina was first a daughter colony of the
           Glueckstal Colonies that was founded just south of
           Hoffnungstal.  Later the village was part of the Hoffnungstal
           parish.  All the years in the St. Petersburg reports are
           included in this one file.  This information was extracted by
           Michael Rempfer and input by Judith Walker in 1995 from the
           St. Petersburg Lutheran Evangelical archives published by
           the LDS.  Edited by Marty McMahon.  -mr (5-31-1996)
