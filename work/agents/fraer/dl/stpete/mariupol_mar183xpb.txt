           This file contains birth records from the Mariupol
           Colonies, South Russia before 1830. This information was
           obtained from the marriage records for the years 1833
           to 1836.  In the interest of compatibility, the ...thal
           suffixes of village names are recorded as ...tal.
           This information was compiled by Tim Wahl in 2009
           from the St. Petersburg Lutheran Evangelical archives
           published by the LDS.

           Special thanks are due to Allen Konrad for motivating
           this work and assisting with the proofing.
           -re (7-11-2009)
