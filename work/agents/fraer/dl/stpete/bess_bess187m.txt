        This file contains marriage records from the Bessarabian
        villages of South Russia for the decade 1870-1879.  This
        information was compiled by R. Reuben Drefs, coordinator,
        and volunteers Ed Schulz and Tom and Jan Stangl January
        26, 1997 from the St. Petersburg Lutheran Evangelical
        archives published by the LDS.  Editing was done by Marty
        McMahon.  This work may be freely copied for nonprofit
        purposes.  -re (10-20-1997)

The Bessarabian marriage extraction files are organized by decade and have
been posted in the same manner. The data provided has only been sorted by
Groom Surname.  The film/item, page and Register numbers are provided to
assist researchers, if they wish to view the actual record found on the
microfilm of the Church of Latter Day Saints [LDS], Family History Library,
Salt Lake City, Utah.  These films can be rented though local LDS Family
History Centers, where they exist.  Some records have Remarks attached to them
which often [especially the 183x decade] provide detailed family information.
There are approximately 11,000 marriage records in this database.  Some
duplicate records exist, since a marriage may have been reported in two
parishes or in two different reporting years.

Most of the St. Petersburg Lutheran Consistory Archive records were organized
and reported by Parish rather than by individual colony.  This is especially
true for the Marriage Records.  Therefore, your ancestors' marriage, while it
probably occurred in their home colony, will usually be reported as if it
occurred elsewhere.  The usual Bessarabian parishes found in the records were
[in order of appearance]:  Sarata, Arcis, Fere Champenoise [Alt-Elft],
Tarutino, and Kloestitz. The smaller parishes of Kischinew [Kischineff] [1844+]
and Benkendorf [1869+] also appear later in the records.

There are gaps in the St. Petersburg records--entire years and some individual
parishes in other years.  The missing data can often be found on local colony
and parish records on other films taken by the LDS Church.  It is planned to
fill in the gaps by researching these other films, but that effort is in its
early stages.  When it is completed, these other records will be added to the
Bessarabian Marriage database and a Volume II hardcopy will be produced.  The
gaps identified are as follows [by Parish]:

   Sarata Parish - all records before 1833; 1844 [23 Nov 1843-22 Jan 1845];
   1849 [2 Dec 1848-27 Dec 1849]; 1865-1866 [13 Dec 1864-5 Jan 1867]; 1872-1874
   [2 Dec 1871-23 Jan 1875];1880 [30 Nov 1879-11 Dec 1880]; all after Dec 1885.
      
   Arcis Parish - all records before 1833; 1844 [25 Nov 1843-4 Jan 1845]; 1849
   [16 Dec 1848-29 Dec 1849]; 1865-1866 [31 Dec 1864-5 Jan 1867]; 1872-1873
   [30 Dec 1871-3 Jan 1874]; 1880 [27 Dec 1879-2 Jan 1881]; all after Dec 1885.

   Fere Champenoise Parish - all records before 1833; 1844 [25 Nov 1843-4 Jan 1845];
   1849 [2 Dec 1848-1 Dec 1849]; 1865-1866 [4 Dec 1864-30 Dec 1866]; 1869 [27 Dec 1868
   -1 Jan 1870]; 1872-1873 [3 Dec 1871-7 Feb 1874]; 1880 [12 Nov 1879-2 Jan 1881];
   all after Dec 1885.

   Tarutino Parish - all records before 1833; 1842 [4 Dec 1841-27 Dec 1842];
   1844 [16 Dec 1843-28 Dec 1844]; 1849 [25 Nov 1848-27 Nov 1849]; 1865-1866
   [18 Dec 1864-22 Jan 1867]; 1872-1873 [late Dec 1871-28 Dec 1873]; 1880
   [29 Dec 1879-29 Dec 1880]; all after Dec 1885.
  
   Kloestitz Parish - all records before 1833; 1842 [29 Dec 1841-5 Jan 1843];
   1844 [9 Dec 1843-28 Dec 1844]; 1849 [2 Dec 1848-22 Dec 1849]; 1864-1866
   [28 Nov 1863-1 Dec 1866]; 1872-1873 [19 Dec 1871-3 Jan 1874]; 1880
   [29 Dec 1879-1 Jan 1881]; all after Dec 1885.

   Kischinew Parish - all records before 1844; most years until 1863; 1872-1873
   [26 Dec 1871-14 Jan 1874]; 1880 [11 Dec 1879-10 Feb 1881]; all after Dec 1885.

   Benkendorf Parish - all records before 1869; 1872-1873 [26 Dec 1871-31 Dec 1873];
   1877-1878 [28 Dec 1876-4 Jan 1879]; 1880 [30 Dec 1879-26 Sep 1880]; all after
   Dec 1885.

Family name spellings often "evolved" through time; therefore, surname
spellings have NOT been standardized, but appear as translated from the German
script by the extractors.  The accuracy of the translations is affected by the
penmanship of the scribes who wrote the records, as well as the fact that
these records were transcribed from local church records, thereby adding to the
possibility of spelling errors.
