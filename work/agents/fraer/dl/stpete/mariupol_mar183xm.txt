           Colonies, South Russia for the decade 183x. In the
           interest of compatibility, the ...thal suffixes of
           village names are recorded as ...tal.
           This information was compiled by Tim Wahl in 2009
           from the St. Petersburg Lutheran Evangelical archives
           published by the LDS.

           this work and assisting with the proofing.
