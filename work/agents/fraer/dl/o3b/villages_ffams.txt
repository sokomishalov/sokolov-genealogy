This file contains a listing of the founding families of Friedental, Crimea,
Russia. 

Information contained in this file was taken from Dr. Karl Stumpp's book, "The 
Emigration from Germany in the Years 1762-1862," records contained in the St. 
Petersburg Consistory films, and other various sources.
