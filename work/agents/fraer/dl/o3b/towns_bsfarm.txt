                                     NOTES

If you will go to the "Essays and Commentary" section of the Odessa Library, you 
will find a translation of the Preface to this book by Roland M. Wagner, and 
also a 2nd article that provides a "Translators Forward" to this great work.  So 
that you will have a better appreciation of this book, it is highly recommended 
that the user of these tables read these two articles.

The following index data has been taken from the book, Deutsche Bauernleistung 
am Schwarzen Meer, Bevoelkerung und Wirtschaft 1825, S. Hirzel Verlag, Leipzig 
1940, by Hans Rempel, Sammlung Georg Leibbrandt.  Translated this title reads, 
"German Farming Achievements in the Black Sea Region, Population and Economy, 
1825".  All 37 tables are provided here.

This book has now become so scarce as to be classified as rare in Germany - the 
GRHS Clearing House went on a search for a copy so an indexing could be made to 
share with the Black Sea German Russian researcher.

Since this book has become so hard to find, you will find that this indexing has 
went a little further than the normal index, and the basic tabled data is 
provided below as much as it can be as it appears in the book.

This project undertaken by the GRHS Clearing House yields great background 
statistics for the Black Sea area in 1825 and in 1826.  If possible we will also 
ensure that other parts of this book will also be made available to the public 
as we are able to find volunteers to translate and prepare such.

The user of this data should be aware that not all this data is perfect within 
the book nor in the work below as it has been taken to the best of our ability 
to reflect here for the researcher of the Black Sea Germans.  We have attempted 
to remove all typing errors we have introduced, but do not depend on it being a 
perfect work.

Of course name spelling and reading of the original records when the data was 
prepared for the book is not always consistent with the spelling of the names 
which we are most familiar.  So we must be careful not to overlook our own data 
when we use these tables.

However, for the seasoned German Russian researcher, there are many pieces of 
treasure to be found in these data files, and in some cases, those working on 
their family history will find some significant data that relates to their 
particular families to include in their own family history as they document 
such. So be alert and attentive and see what these files hold for you.

We owe great thanks to the following people who assisted in the 
preparation of these tables and the data that is contained there-in;

   Diane Martin
   Elli Wise
   Ralph Wiseman
   David Kilwien
   Roland M. Wagner
