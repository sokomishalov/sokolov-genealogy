This file contains Roman Catholic Baptisms from the parish of Simferopol, 
Crimea, Russia and includes the villages of Rosental, Kronental, and 
Friedental, as well as other villages which had Roman Catholic inhabitants. The 
film number is #2009092 and also #2010574 and includes the years of 1886-1891. 
These two films contain the same information. 

The information on these films are in Russian Cryllic and was translated by 
Aubrey. Edited by Marty McMahon.

This file is posted in memory of Aubrey's deceased father, John Henry 
Marthaller, on this the 93rd anniversary of his birth in Bowdle, South Dakota.
