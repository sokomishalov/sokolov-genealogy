This file contains Roman Catholic Baptisms from the parish of Rosental, Crimea, 
Russia and includes the villages of Rosental, Kronental, and Friedental, as 
well as other villages which had Roman Catholic inhabitants. The film number is 
#2008422 and includes the years of 1847-1865. 

The information on this film is in Russian Cryllic and was translated by 
Aubrey. Edited by Marty McMahon.

This file is posted in memory of Aubrey's deceased father, John Henry 
Marthaller, on this the 93rd anniversary of his birth in Bowdle, South Dakota.
