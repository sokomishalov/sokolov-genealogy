German Russian Peoples who came to
CHEYENNE COUNTY KANSAS

Paul Reeb was born in Colorado but spent most of his life in Cheyenne County
Kansas.  Paul was an early member of the original AHSGR and was one of the
South Russian German Russians who helped establish the GRHS.

Paul is the person who made a major project out of defining the term "German
Russian."

One of his major projects was worked on for about three years.  And the
results of those three years are reflected below.  He spent many hours going
through the county records and making notes on arrivals of German Russians to
Cheyenne county.  Some do not feel his work was complete, but having reviewed
those notes (I, Dale Wahl) conclude that the work is almost complete.  Penny
Raile of St. Francis has also went through these records and perhaps we can
some day post a copy of her extractions also.

You will find the results of Paul's list has been provided freely in many
forms.  Even on one occasion, it was published in a book about Cheyenne county
(Cheyenne County History, published in 1987 by The Cheyenne County Historical
Society.)  If you take that list and the one below, you may discover some
basic differences, but you should note that both lists come from the same set
of notes that Paul took during this project.  As far as I can recall, those
with an NE entry in the From Where or Arrived fields went to Nebraska and
then to Kansas.

Please feel free to use this data of Paul's, and please give due credit if you
should use it somewhere in your family history.  (This data is shared with
permission of the cousin whom Paul left his family information.)

"He who cares not whence he came,
cares not whither he goes."
         Paul Reeb, 25 June 1969

(If you get close to St. Francis KS in your travels, make sure you stop and
look through the Museum there, the spirit of Paul is still alive in the
community.)

Note: Entries in the "From Where and "Arrived" fields marked NE are presumed
      to refer to people who came to St. Francis by way of Nebraska. - DW
