Original German Settlers in the village of Koenigsbach in Poland - 1804
Translation by - Thomas A. Stangl, Sterling, VA - Feb 1998

The following tables were translated/extracted from an excerpt from--"Mundart
und Siedlungsgeschichte der schwaebisch-reinfraenkischen Doerfer bei
Litzmannstadt" [Lodz] written by Michael Schmit, published in Deutsche
Dialektgeographie, Heft XLI, Marburg, 1942.  The excerpt covers pages 202-236
of Schmit, which describe the German village of Koenigsbach, Poland. The
Polish name for this village is Bukowiec.  It was located southeast of the
city of Lodz, Poland.  The descriptive material was not translated.  Two
tables of colonist names were included in the narrative and are translated
below.

The Bessarabian Colony column was NOT in the original document, but was
developed by the translator by consulting other sources.  While records on the
specifically named colonist may NOT actually be found in Bessarabia, the
SURNAME/ place-of-origin is definitely found in the listed Bessarabian
colony.  If the surname is found in several Bessarabian colonies,   it is
listed as "Present."  Unless good evidence exists that the surname listed is
in a Bessarabian colony, "None" is indicated.  If a surname/place-of-origin
is found in Odessa, the colony is indicated in [  ].  Of the original families
listed in the first table, the following House numbers later moved to other
villages: 1-4, 6-8, 12-16, 18, 19, 21-29, 31, 33-35, 37-47, 51, 54, 55, 63.
