Data from German-Russians, extracted from Prairie Echoes of Hatton - A Story of
Hatton, Saskatchewan and Surrounding Area, published in 1983

Anhorn, Emanuel      born 1884 Bess Russia died 1966; married 1909 Katherine Anhorn (no relation) died 1965 age 76; immigrated to Canada 1910.
Anhorn, Gotlieb      & Katherine  emigrated from Russia to Canada 1910 with 5 children: Gotlieb Jr, Katherine, Adolph, Bertha & Emanuel all b Alt Postale Romania.
Anhorn, Gottlieb     & Katherine Sr  emigrated from Akerman Russia 1911.
Bayer, Reinhold      born 1882 Sarata Bess Russia died 1963; wife Maria Gessler born 1886 Sarata Bess Russia died 1963; immigrated to Canada 1911.
Brusky, Peter        born 1887 Parkston SD; immigrated with brother Frank to Canada 1910; married Martha Doll born 1897 Marshfield, Wisconsin.
Busse, Rev. William  born 1895 died 1976 Hopkins Minnesota; Pastor of Hatton Lutheran Church 1927 mid 30s; wife Gertrude;
Diebel, Harry K      born 1900 Milton ND; immigrated to Canada c1912; married 1917 Minnie Clara Price born 1896 Wisconsin.
Drockner, Gus        born 1910 Kauna Lith Russia; immigrated to Canada 1929; married Freda Scherer.
Elhard, Jacob        born 1897 Jud ND son of Jacob & Juliana (Mogck); immigrated to Canada 1909; married 1917 Emelia Ott.
Faas, Fred           born 1884 Bess Russia died 1940; wife Marie Schulz born 1885 Bess Russia; immigrated to US; married 1905 WA State; immigrated to Canada 1910; Marie married 2) Gottfried Funk American Falls, ID died 1970.
Fischer, John B      & wife emigrated from Bess to US 1907; married 1910 & farmed at Linden ND; immigrated to Canada 1917.
Gnam, Christ         & Margaret  emigrated from Russia to Canada 1910.
Gutknecht, Karl      wife Paulina died 1924; emigrated from Russia to SK 1912
Hahn, Philip         & Sophia (Vorreitor) died 1975 age 85 both b Russia; immigrated to Canada 1911
Heck, Mathias        born 1888 Russia died 1966; immigrated to Canada 1908; married Carolina Backmeir died 1971.
Heine, Gottlieb      born 1877 Tarutina Russia died 1931; married 1902 Maria Sept born 1879 Alt Bostahl Bess died 1956; immigrated to Canada 1911.
Hepper, Henry R      born 1893 Amor SD son of Henry Sr & Katherine; emigrated from ND 1906; married 1917 Marie Ott daughter of Jacob & Magdalina.
Hoffman, Gottlieb    born 1900 SD died 1980 son of Anton & Maria; immigrated to Canada 1906; married 1929 Melita Schuler born 1913 daughter of Gottlieb & Anna.
Hoffman, Paul        born 1905 Sykeston ND died 1965 son of Anton & Maria; immigrated to Canada 1907; married 1937 Lydia Fischer born 1910 daughter of John & Wilhelmina; immigrated to Canada 1917.
Jans, Daniel         born 1880 Romania Russia died 1971; immigrated to Canada with wife & 3 children; married 2) 1929 Magdaline Gulschatzke.
Jans, Fred           & Annie (Schlag) b Russia.
Kramer, Arthur       born 1926 son of Joseph & Caroline (Backmeir).
Loch, Pauline        born 1889 Germany; emigrated from Russia to Canada 1909.
Maier, Fred          died 1952 & Katie (Ketterling) emigrated from US 1913. See Wishek Golden Jubilee Book, Adam Ketterling p108
Maier, Henry         born 1876 Russia died 1958; married 1) 1898 Leisa Klein died 1910; immigrated to Mott ND before 1910; immigrated to Canada 1911; married 2) widow Emma Bechold died 1920; married 3) widow Salome Brothers died 1970.
Maves, Paul          died 1953 age 77 & Louisa Hinz died 1979 age 96 married 1900 Grafton ND; farmed at Epworth ND.
Moch, Gottlieb       born 1900 Bess Russia son of Valentine & Rosina; immigrated to Canada 1909; married 1923 Florntina Traxel born 1905 Tripp SD; immigrated to Canada 1912.
Moch, Henry          b Russia; married Ruth Harkness.
Moch, Leonard        born 1912 Hatton SK married 1937 Clara Brusky born 1920 daughter of Frank & Katie. See Golden Prairie book.
Moch, Philip         b 1914 Hatton SK; married 1936 Freda Fode.
Moch, Valentine      died 1935 & Rosina (Schuler) died 1948 from Russia; emigrated from US to Canada 1909.
Ott, Jacob Sr        b Grosliebentahl Russia; immigrated to ND c1904; wife Magdaline died 1920.
Pfaff, Gottlieb      died 1941 age 82 & wife 1) Margaret died 1908 emigrated from Russia 1905; wife 2) Katherina* Siegle died 1942 age 69.
Phaff, John*         born 1901 son of Gottlieb & Margaret; married 1923 Florence Stahl born 1904 Bowbells ND daughter of Herman & Clara, immigrated to Canada 1911. *daughter Laura compiled this book.
Rinke, Christen      died 1939 & Caroline (Klaudt) died 1949 age 62; emigrated from Russia 1910.
Scherer, Edward      born 1909 Jameburg ND son of Ludwig; immigrated to Canada 1913; married 1930 Edna German born 1909 Oxford ND daughter of Al; came to Canada 1911.
Scherer, Emanuel     born 1902 ND died 1965; married 1927 Christina Jans born 1909 Russia daughter of Daniel Sr.
Schmidt, Adam Sr     born 1872 Russia died 1940; married 1897 Ottellia Kemph born 1880 Bess died 1962; immigrated to Canada 1911.
Schuler, Gottlieb    born 1884 Russia died 1963; married Anna born 1891 Russia died 1929; moved to Hanna AB. *Came from Worms (?) Russia with Fred Meyer (grandfather to MLH) & Henry Schmidt 1904.
Sept, Edward         born 1898 ND died 1972; immigrated to Canada c1902; married 1920 Freda Wuest born 1900 Switzerland, immigrated to Canada 1910.
Sept, Jacob          born 1888 Bostall Bess Russia died 1949 son of George & Christina (Albrecht); immigrated to Canada 1902; married 1924 Amailia (Molly) Moch b Russia daughter of Valentine & Rosina.
Siegle, David        born 1886 Hoffnungstal Russia died 1968; married 1910 Christine Holzwarth born 1886 Russia died 1977; immigrated to Canada 1911.
Singer, Jake         emigrated from Russia; wife ________Gehring.
Stahl, Albert        1915 son of Herman b Germany & Clara (Harbig), immigrated to Canada 1911; married 1937 Norma Willis.
Stahl, Alfred        born 1903 died 1980; emigrated from Bowbells ND 1910; married 1) 1926 Anna Fischer died 1930 daughter John B Fischer; 2) 1931 Rebecca Fischer b 1912 Wishek ND sis to Anna, immigrated to Canada 1917.
Stahl, Edward        from Bowbells ND; married 1935 Elsie Anhorn died 1965.
Stuber, Fredrick     born 1893 Albota Bess Russia died 1969; immigrated to US & Canada 1913; married 1918 Maria Hoffman born 1895 Grim SRussia died 1978 daughter of Anton & Maria, immigrated to US 1898; from ND to Canada 1906.
Weiss, Pauline       born 1908 Lehr ND daughter of David; immigrated to Canada 1910.
Werk, Daniel         born 1880 Russia so of Gottlieb; immigrated to Canada 1903; married 1909 Mollie Breen.
Werre, Gottfried     born 1873 Ireland died 1931; married 1902 @ Eureka SD Eva Huber born 1883 Odessa Russia died 1964; immigrated to Canada 1911.
