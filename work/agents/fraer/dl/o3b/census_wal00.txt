Note: The data source is National Archives film T623.1555.  The data in
this census is unreliable and should be used with caution.  Handwriting
is poor, the document is damaged and taped, the enumerators spell the
German names badly, and the data itself is bad.  Many families were
enumerated twice, and there is inconsistency between the double
entries.
